### Türkçe

Ehlibyte, kültürel değerlere ve ortak ilgi alanlarına dayalı akıllı eşleştirme sistemi ile seni doğru insanlarla buluşturan modern bir sosyal platformdur. 💬 **Mesajlaşma ve Bağlantı**

Ehlibyte ile kültürel değerlerine uygun, anlamlı bağlantılar kur. Türk kültürünün zenginliğini modern bir platformda yaşa.

### İngilizce

Ehlibyte is a modern social platform that connects you with the right people through smart matching based on cultural values and shared interests. Build compatible connections with Turkish culture-specific signals (folk songs, poets, hometown), chat in Meydan live voice rooms, and share in the community feed.

**Key Features:**

🔍 **Smart Cultural Matching**
- Culture, interests, and lifestyle compatibility scores
- Hometown, favorite poet, and folk song signals
- Location-based nearby matches
- Tinder-like swipe system

🎙️ **Meydan Live Voice Rooms**
- Virtual Cem room experience
- Real-time voice chat
- Live video broadcast participation
- Live interaction and community

📱 **Community Feed**
- Folk songs, questions, and trending discussions
- Polls and link previews
- Likes, comments, and shares
- Muharrem month special content

🔥 **Fire and Profile Boost**
- Stand out by sending Fire
- Boost your profile for 24 hours in discovery
- Premium users get priority matching
- Fire packages and features

💬 **Messaging and Connection**
- Instant messaging after matching
- Distance display
- Conversation starters with profile prompts
- Spotify integration

🛡️ **Safety and Controls**
- Block, mute, and report features
- Profile verification and badges
- Safe matching rules
- Admin moderation panel

**Premium Features:**
- Unlimited swipes
- See who likes you
- Location-based matching
- Undo feature
- Ad-free experience
- Profile boost and Fire features

With Ehlibyte, build meaningful connections that align with your cultural values. Experience the richness of Turkish culture on a modern platform.
